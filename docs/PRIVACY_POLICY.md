## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Clubnizer ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, shares, and protects personal data when you use our platform for sports club management ("Service").

We are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your data responsibly. This policy applies to all users of Clubnizer, including Club administrators, members, and guardians.

## 2. Data Controller vs Data Processor Roles

Understanding who is responsible for your data depends on the context:

### 2.1 Club Data (Clubnizer as Data Processor)

**For data processed within Clubs**, the Club acts as the **Data Controller**. This means the Club (through its administrators) determines why and how member data is processed within their organization.

Clubnizer acts as a **Data Processor**, processing Club data only on behalf of and under the instructions of the Club. We do not independently determine the purposes of processing Club member data.

### 2.2 Account Data (Clubnizer as Data Controller)

**For data related to your Clubnizer account** (such as your login credentials and account settings), Clubnizer acts as the **Data Controller** and processes such data to provide and maintain the Service.

### 3.9 Swedish Personal Identity Number (Personnummer)

Providing your personal identity number (_personnummer_) is **entirely optional**. It is only required if your club uses the LOK attendance reporting feature to submit activity data to RF in order to qualify for activity-based public funding (_aktivitetsstöd_).

If you choose to provide your personnummer, we collect:

The full personnummer is **never stored in plaintext**. See Section 12b for a full description of how it is protected.

## 9. Data Retention

### 9.1 Active Accounts

We retain your data while your account remains active and as necessary to provide the Service.

### 9.2 Inactive Memberships

When you leave a group, are removed from a Club, or your membership becomes inactive:

- Your membership record is **soft-deleted** (marked as inactive)
- A **30-day grace period** applies, during which a Club administrator can reactivate your membership
- After 30 days, inactive membership records are permanently deleted by an automated cleanup process
- Any outstanding membership fees associated with the membership are cancelled upon removal

### 9.3 Account Deletion ("Delete My Account")

You can delete your own account at any time using the **"Delete My Account"** option in your Profile settings within the app. Account deletion is **immediate and permanent** — there is no recovery period.

#### Pre-conditions

Before you can delete your account, you must:

1. **Leave all groups** — you cannot have any active group memberships
2. **Not hold a club-admin role** — another club admin must remove you as club admin first
3. **Not be a guardian of active youth players** — contact a club admin to remove the guardian association first

If any of these conditions are not met, the app will inform you of the specific steps required.

#### What is deleted immediately

- Your **Firebase Authentication account** (login credentials)
- Your **user profile document** (name, email, phone, profile photo reference, preferences)
- Your **in-app notifications**

#### What is NOT deleted by account deletion

- **Club membership records** — these are managed by the Club (as Data Controller) and follow the inactive membership lifecycle described in Section 9.2. Since you must leave all groups before deleting your account, these records will already be in the soft-deleted state.
- **Event and attendance data** — historical event responses and attendance records you contributed while active may be retained by the Club for administrative purposes.
- **Payment and transaction records** — retained as required by law and by our payment processor (Stripe) for financial compliance.
- **Anonymized usage data** — aggregated, non-identifiable analytics data may be retained.

### 9.3a Admin-Deactivated Accounts

If your account is deactivated by an administrator (rather than self-deleted), a **60-day grace period** applies. During this period, the account may be reactivated. After 60 days, the account is permanently deleted by an automated cleanup process.

### 9.4 Extended Retention

We may retain certain data longer if:

- Required by law (e.g., financial records)
- Necessary for legitimate business purposes (e.g., fraud prevention)
- Subject to ongoing disputes or legal proceedings

### 9.5 Backup Retention

Backups may retain data for a limited period after deletion from the primary system.

### 9.6 Personnummer Retention (Swedish Clubs)

Your personnummer is retained only for as long as you have given consent. You may withdraw consent and permanently delete your stored personnummer at any time via **LOK Settings → Withdraw Consent** in the app. Deletion is **immediate and permanent** — the encrypted value is removed from the database with no grace period.

Your personnummer is also permanently deleted when you delete your account (Section 9.3). It is **not** subject to the soft-delete grace period that applies to membership records.

## 12b. Special Handling of Swedish Personal Identity Numbers (Personnummer)

> **This section applies only to Swedish clubs using the LOK Attendance Reporting feature.**

We take particular care with Swedish personal identity numbers given their sensitivity under Swedish law (_dataskyddslagen 3 kap. 10 §_). The following security measures apply specifically to personnummer:

### Entry and Validation

Your personnummer is entered directly in the app and transmitted over an encrypted connection (HTTPS/TLS) to our backend. Format and Luhn checksum validation are performed **server-side** before any storage occurs. The number is never processed or stored on the client device.

### Encryption

Your personnummer is encrypted server-side using **AES-256-GCM** (Advanced Encryption Standard with a 256-bit key in Galois/Counter Mode — an authenticated encryption algorithm). Each encryption operation uses a unique, cryptographically random initialization vector (IV), ensuring that the same personnummer encrypted twice produces different ciphertext.

### Key Management

The encryption key is stored exclusively in **Google Cloud Secret Manager**. It is never embedded in source code, in environment files accessible to client applications, or in the Firestore database. Access to the key is restricted to the specific backend functions that require it.

### What Is Stored

Only the following is written to the database:

- The **encrypted ciphertext** (never the plaintext personnummer)
- The **birth date portion** (first 8 digits, e.g. `19900101`) — this non-sensitive derived field is used for age-eligibility checks without requiring decryption
- Consent metadata: timestamp of when consent was given and the version of the policy consented to

### Client Access

The full personnummer is **never returned to the client application**. The app can only receive confirmation that a number is stored along with the birth date — not the personnummer digits themselves.

### Decryption Scope

Decryption occurs exclusively on the backend, only during LOK report generation for automated submission to the Swedish Sports Confederation (RF). Decryption is not available as a user-callable or client-callable operation.

### Right to Withdraw

You can delete your personnummer at any time via **LOK Settings → Withdraw Consent** in the app. This immediately and permanently removes the encrypted value from the database. See Section 9.6.
