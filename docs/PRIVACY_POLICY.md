## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Sportnizer ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, shares, and protects personal data when you use our platform for sports club management ("Service").

We are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your data responsibly. This policy applies to all users of Sportnizer, including Club administrators, members, and guardians.

---

## 2. Data Controller vs Data Processor Roles

Understanding who is responsible for your data depends on the context:

### 2.1 Club Data (Sportnizer as Data Processor)

**For data processed within Clubs**, the Club acts as the **Data Controller**. This means the Club (through its administrators) determines why and how member data is processed within their organization.

Sportnizer acts as a **Data Processor**, processing Club data only on behalf of and under the instructions of the Club. We do not independently determine the purposes of processing Club member data.

### 2.2 Account Data (Sportnizer as Data Controller)

**For data related to your Sportnizer account** (such as your login credentials and account settings), Sportnizer acts as the **Data Controller** and processes such data to provide and maintain the Service.

## 3. Data We Collect

### 3.1 Account Data

When you create an account, we collect:

- Email address
- Display name
- Password (stored as a secure hash)

### 3.2 Profile Data

You may optionally provide:

- Profile photo
- Phone number
- Date of birth (required for Youth Members)

### 3.3 Club Membership Data

When you join or are added to a Club, we collect:

- Club and group associations
- Roles within the Club (e.g., player, coach, admin)
- Guardian relationships (for Youth Members)

### 3.4 Youth Member Data

For Youth Members (users under 16), we collect:

- Name
- Date of birth
- Guardian contact information

**Note:** Clubs are responsible for obtaining guardian consent before adding Youth Members. Sportnizer does not independently verify that such consent has been obtained.

### 3.5 Event and Attendance Data

When you participate in events, we collect:

- Event RSVPs and responses
- Attendance records
- Participation history for statistical purposes

### 3.6 Payment Data

When payments are processed through the Platform:

- Stripe processes payment card details
- We store transaction records and payment status
- We do not store full credit card numbers

### 3.7 Usage Data

We automatically collect:

- App interactions and feature usage
- Device information (platform, app version)
- Error logs and performance data

This data helps us improve the Service and troubleshoot issues.

## 9. Data Retention

### 9.1 Active Accounts

We retain your data while your account remains active and as necessary to provide the Service.

### 9.2 Inactive Memberships

When you are removed from a Club or your membership becomes inactive:

- A **30-day grace period** applies before data deletion
- During this period, your membership can be reactivated
- After 30 days, membership data may be permanently deleted

### 9.3 Deleted Accounts

When you delete your account:

- A **30-day recovery period** applies
- You may reactivate your account during this period
- After 30 days, account data is permanently deleted

### 9.4 Extended Retention

We may retain certain data longer if:

- Required by law (e.g., financial records)
- Necessary for legitimate business purposes (e.g., fraud prevention)
- Subject to ongoing disputes or legal proceedings

### 9.5 Backup Retention

Backups may retain data for a limited period after deletion from the primary system.
